## 2. Interactive Navigation Guide for Evaluators

The live application is structured into 5 dedicated executive modules, accessible via the top navigation bar:

1. **Tab 1: PSK.hr Live Prototype (Deliverable D1)**
   - Experience the working Croatian sports betting interface (`PSK.hr`).
   - Switch between **Express Matchday**, **Tactical Analytics**, and **In-Play Momentum** using the Adaptive Intent Lens.
   - Click odds on the Dinamo vs. Hajduk derby to open the **Zero-Pressure Reassurance Betslip**.
   - Observe real-time Croatian statutory tax breakdown (10% tax on net winnings) and self-set budget indicators.
   - Try the soft-exit options: *"Save to Match Watchlist"* or *"Track Odds Shifts"*.

2. **Tab 2: Interactive Scenarios (Deliverable D2)**
   - Step through the 3 simulated user journeys:
     - **Marco (Zagreb):** High-intent football fan exploring the Eternal Derby under Express mode.
     - **Luka (Split):** Hesitant, price-sensitive punter experiencing zero-pressure tax transparency.
     - **Elena (Rijeka):** Responsible gaming guardrail test demonstrating velocity detection and limit protection.
   - Click **"Next Step"** to walk through the before-and-after contrast for each persona.

3. **Tab 3: Business Impact Model (Deliverable D3)**
   - Real-time dynamic financial calculator modeling **€3.52M in annual net GGR uplift**.
   - Review 5-market CEE reconciliation across Croatia, Czechia, Slovakia, Poland, and Romania.
   - Adjust baseline sessions or conversion uplift sliders to view sensitivity analysis in real time.
   - Inspect FEG India GCC implementation costs (€225k CapEx/OpEx) and **23.3-day payback period**.

4. **Tab 4: Compliance Note (Deliverable D4)**
   - Formal audit note outlining compliance with Croatian Gambling Act (NN 87/08), Czech Act 186/2016, GDPR, and EGBA responsible gambling standards.

5. **Tab 5: Presentation Deck**
   - 9-slide executive pitch deck with word-for-word speaker notes right underneath each slide.
